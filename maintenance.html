Asset Care & Operations

MEP Maintenance & Facility Management

Comprehensive preventive, predictive, and emergency maintenance services for MEP assets to ensure long-term operational performance and minimum downtime.

Service Overview

Sustainable Gate Electromechanical Works LLC delivers end-to-end facility maintenance contracts (PPM and SLA-based) for commercial properties, residential communities, and industrial facilities across the UAE. Our team of certified technicians ensures your HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and low-current systems remain energy-efficient, compliant, and continuously operational.

Key Offerings Included

Planned Preventive Maintenance (PPM)

Scheduled quarterly and annual system servicing to reduce unexpected component failures and prolong asset life.

24/7 Emergency Call-out Response

Rapid response dispatch for critical electrical power outages, pipe bursts, and central AC cooling failures.

HVAC & Chiller Plant Overhaul

Duct cleaning, compressor maintenance, refrigerant top-ups, and AHU/FCU coil servicing.

Electrical & Plumbing System Repairs

MDB/SMDB panel inspections, thermographic testing, pump overhauls, and water supply network repairs.

Our Facility Maintenance Process

01

Asset Audit & Condition Assessment

Evaluating existing MEP machinery, establishing baseline operational health, and logging asset tags.

02

Custom PPM Schedule Creation

Designing tailored maintenance calendars adhering to manufacturer specifications and authority guidelines.

03

Execution & Digital Reporting

Conducting scheduled system inspections, part replacements, and issuing digital maintenance logs.

04

Performance Monitoring & Optimization

Analyzing energy usage trends, reducing operational costs, and recommending proactive asset renewals.
