Specialized Trade Engineering

Electrical & Power Engineering Services

Turnkey high, medium, and low voltage electrical installation, power distribution networks, panel manufacturing coordination, and DEWA/SEWA compliance solutions.

Service Overview

Sustainable Gate Electromechanical Works LLC delivers comprehensive electrical engineering services for commercial, residential, and industrial facilities across the UAE. From main distribution boards (MDBs) and transformer installations to interior architectural lighting and authority inspection clearances, our licensed engineers ensure maximum safety, energy efficiency, and strict adherence to DEWA, SEWA, and ADDC electrical regulations.

Key Offerings Included

Power Distribution & Switchgear Systems

Installation of MDBs, SMDBs, Final Distribution Boards, busbar trunking networks, and power factor correction panels.

Lighting & Smart Control Solutions

Energy-efficient LED lighting, emergency/exit lighting networks, and DALI/KNX automated lighting controls.

Earthing & Lightning Protection

Comprehensive earth pit installations, bonding systems, and structural surge/lightning protection networks.

Backup Power & Renewable Integrations

Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) systems, diesel generator installations, and solar PV power grid connections.

Our Electrical Execution Process

01

Load Analysis & Authority Approvals

Calculating connected load schedules, single-line diagrams (SLDs), and securing DEWA/SEWA NOC clearances.

02

Containment & Cabling Execution

Routing heavy-duty cable trays, conduits, trunking, and pulling XLPE armored power cables across zones.

03

Panel Termination & Fixture Installation

Mounting and terminating distribution boards, light fittings, power outlets, switches, and central control gear.

04

Testing, Commissioning & Energization

Insulation resistance testing, megger testing, phase rotation checks, authority meter installation, and energization.
