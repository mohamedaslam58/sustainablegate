Specialized Trade Engineering

Plumbing & Public Health Engineering

Comprehensive water supply, drainage networks, stormwater management, sanitary installations, and local municipality-approved plumbing solutions.

Service Overview

Sustainable Gate Electromechanical Works LLC delivers end-to-end public health and plumbing engineering services across commercial, residential, and industrial developments in the UAE. Our engineering teams specialize in high-efficiency domestic water distribution, sustainable drainage piping networks, central water heating, and wastewater treatment integration in full compliance with DEWA and local municipality standards.

Key Offerings Included

Domestic Water Supply Systems

Piping networks (PPR, PEX, Copper), pressure booster pump sets, central filtration, and water storage tank installations.

Drainage & Venting Systems

Soil, waste, and vent piping (UPVC, HDPE, Cast Iron), grease traps, acoustic insulation, and lifting station pumps.

Stormwater & Rainwater Drainage

Roof drainage collection, siphonic systems, attenuation tanks, and external site storm network management.

Central Hot Water & Solar Integration

Central water heaters, heat pumps, circulation pumps, and solar water heating plant installations.

Our Plumbing Execution Process

01

System Hydraulics & Design Approvals

Calculating pipe sizing, fixture units, pump head loss, and obtaining local municipal approvals.

02

Underground & First Fix Installation

Laying underground drainage networks, wall chases, riser stacks, and sleeve penetrations.

03

Second Fix & Plant Room Setup

Installing water pumps, storage tanks, water heaters, sanitary fixtures, and valve assemblies.

04

Pressure Testing & Chlorination

Conducting hydrostatic pressure tests, gravity drainage flow tests, water disinfection, and authority sign-off.
