Project Services

Tender & Estimating Services

Comprehensive tender preparation, BOQ generation, cost estimation, and technical documentation designed to maximize bid accuracy and competitiveness.

Service Overview

Sustainable Gate Electromechanical Works LLC delivers end-to-end tendering support for commercial, residential, and industrial MEP engineering projects across the UAE. We assist main contractors, developers, and asset owners in producing compliant, accurate, and cost-effective tender submissions.

Key Offerings Included

Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

Detailed material take-offs and cost estimations adhering strictly to standard UAE authority regulations.

Technical Proposal Compilation

Complete compliance statements, method statements, and structural technical submittals.

Commercial Bidding Support

In-depth price analysis, value engineering options, and strategic risk identification.

Vendor & Subcontractor Coordination

Obtaining competitive quotations for specialized equipment and specialized trade materials.
