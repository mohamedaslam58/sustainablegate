Engineering & Consultancy

MEP Design & Engineering Services

Comprehensive mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and low-current design solutions engineered to UAE authority standards, focused on energy efficiency and structural compliance.

Service Overview

Sustainable Gate Electromechanical Works LLC delivers end-to-end MEP engineering design services for commercial, residential, and industrial facilities across the UAE. From concept schematics to authority approvals, our engineering team ensures all layouts prioritize energy efficiency, spatial coordination, and full local authority compliance (DEWA, SEWA, DCD, Municipality).

Key Offerings Included

Conceptual & Detailed Schematic Design

Initial load estimations, system architecture planning, and comprehensive MEP spatial allocation.

Authority Submissions & Approvals

Preparation and NOC processing for DEWA, Civil Defence, Municipality, and local UAE authorities.

3D BIM & Shop Drawing Creation

Clash detection, REVIT 3D modeling, and execution-ready detailed shop drawings.

Green Building & Energy Modeling

Sustainable system design aligning with Al Sa'fat, Estidama, and LEED certification frameworks.

Our Engineering Design Process

01

Requirement Analysis & Load Calculations

Evaluating architectural briefs, conducting thermal cooling calculations, and electrical load assessments.

02

Concept & Schematic Layouts

Developing preliminary single-line diagrams, riser diagrams, and equipment plant space planning.

03

BIM Coordination & Detail Engineering

Conducting multi-disciplinary clash analysis between HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and structural elements.

04

Authority Approval & Issued for Construction (IFC)

Finalizing engineering submittals, securing authority stamps, and issuing coordinated site construction drawings.
